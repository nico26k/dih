## Patterns
- **Streamers playing GTA 5 story mode before launch** (Kai Cenat, Goldbridge, Joe Bart) are working right now.
- **Length:** most winners are 10 to 35 seconds.
- **Title:** `<Streamer> <what happened>` plus 😭/💀/😂. Short and literal.
- **Hooks:** "wait for it", "first streamer to…", rankings.
- Brand-new channels can get tens of thousands of views on their first clips.
