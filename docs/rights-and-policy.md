# Rights & policy — read before posting

Clipping channels live or die on this. These are guidelines, not legal advice — **verify the current policies yourself**, they change.

## Streamer permission
- Stream footage belongs to the streamer. Many big streamers **welcome clippers** (some even run paid clipping programs or Discord channels for it); others file copyright claims.
- Before clipping someone, check their channel panels, socials, or Discord for a clipping policy. When in doubt, ask. Record the answer in `trackers/streamers.csv` (`clip_policy` column).
- If a streamer asks you to stop or take something down — do it immediately.

## YouTube monetization ("reused content")
- YouTube can refuse to monetize channels that re-upload others' content without adding much.
- Add real value to every video: editing, captions, commentary, curation/compilation, context. Raw re-uploads are the risk.
- Always credit and link the streamer in the description.

## Game footage
- Check Rockstar/Take-Two's current policy on gameplay videos and monetization before monetizing.
- Avoid posting leaks or unreleased material.

## Music
- Streamers' background music can trigger Content ID claims. Mute or replace it with royalty-free music.
